Methodology. Built from output/stocks_*.csv — the daily local backups market_pulse.py writes alongside its Google Sheet log. Each segment's daily average 1d/5d/20d % move and volume-vs-20d-average are pooled across all segments and day-to-day transitions to train a ridge-regularized logistic regression (numpy, gradient descent — no new dependency) predicting next-day direction. Backtested on a chronological (not random) holdout of the most recent days, which is the honest way to test a time series. Month-to-date figures reuse each segment's existing trailing-20-day average return.

Limitations. A short data history and few segments is a small sample — read probabilities as a directional lean, not a calibrated forecast. The model sees only price and volume; it does not see the news/policy signals market_pulse.py's daily digest already tracks separately.

Directional research signal derived from price/volume momentum only — not trading or investment advice.
